So for example, if PeachOS has something like:

tss_set_stack(stack);

you can simply turn it into:

set_tss_stack(stack);

That's exactly the sort of translation you're talking about.

But variable order is important

Suppose a PeachOS file has:

tss_set_stack(&tss, stack);

and yours is:

set_tss_stack(stack);

You cannot just rename the function. You need to remove the TSS argument too:

set_tss_stack(stack);

Likewise, if PeachOS has:

tss_write(5, 0x10, stack);

and yours is:

write_tss(5, 0x10, stack);

then that's a direct replacement.
